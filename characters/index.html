How can one resident have different story roles?

Each playthrough selects a supernatural killer from a pool of authored candidates. A resident can therefore be alive, dead, targeted as a victim, or selected as the killer in different campaigns. Their reasoning, kill style, targets, relationship scenes, and survival consequences can change the route.

What character details are not confirmed yet?

The complete cast, all 12 romanceable names, birthdays, daily schedules, loved gifts, heart-event requirements, and campaign-specific alibis are not fully public. Individual profiles distinguish developer-revealed facts from fields that still require verification in the release build.

Frequently asked questions

Character FAQ

Does every Ashenridge resident have a home?

Yes. The developer says all 30-plus characters have unique homes and places to visit.

Can residents visit the docks at sunset?

Yes. The official time devlog gives sunset at the docks as an example of NPC activity.

Can Ashenridge residents drink late at night?

Yes. Late-night drinking is another official example of the full-day NPC schedule.

Is Father Tomás an official Grave Seasons character?

Yes. Perfect Garbage featured Father Tomás in its official 400,000-wishlist update.

Is Yuto an official Grave Seasons character?

Yes. Yuto appeared in the official 300,000-wishlist celebration.

Are reactions to murders animated?

Yes. The developer lists reacting to a murder among the bespoke animated moments supported by player customization.

Are romance kisses animated in Grave Seasons?

Yes. Sharing a kiss with a lover is named as one of the hand-animated player moments.

Do resident quests help build trust?

Yes. Official feature descriptions connect completing quests for townsfolk with earning their trust and learning more about them.

Can a romance candidate become the killer?

Yes. Steam confirms that some of the 12 potential romance options can be selected as the killer in a playthrough.

Can a romance candidate become a victim?

Yes. Official descriptions allow townsfolk to be victims while relationships and survival outcomes continue to shape the story.

Can the same character have different story roles?

Yes. A resident can have different relevance when they are alive, dead, a victim, or the active killer in separate playthroughs.

Does the surviving resident roster change Ashenridge?

Yes. Steam says Ashenridge changes depending on which residents are alive, along with the active killer and current season.

Do character jobs help with investigations?

Yes. The journal example shows that a business-related clue can require knowing which resident works there and where they spend time.

Are all Ashenridge residents romanceable?

No. The game has more than 30 residents but only 12 potential romance options.
