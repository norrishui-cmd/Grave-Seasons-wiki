Commercial District Was the First Non-Farm Area Built

The developer identifies the Commercial District as the first location built after the player farm.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: Locations
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

The June map update describes it as Mayor Fitch's attempted tourism jewel and a reminder of Ashenridge's isolation. It includes commercial businesses, a curated square, and a central artistic fountain.

What this changes for players

The production note gives the district a distinct identity and supports its role as a hub for shops, residents, clues, and yearly events. It also ties Mayor Fitch directly to the area's civic ambition.

What is not confirmed

The final business list, map coordinates, opening hours, event calendar, and all accessible interiors remain unpublished.

Primary source

This update was checked against the official Grave Seasons developer or Steam publication. If a newer first-party announcement changes the fact, this canonical page will be revised rather than duplicated.
