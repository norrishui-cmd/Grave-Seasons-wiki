The Crowbar Remains a Promoted Investigation Tool

Official release messaging tells players to keep the crowbar clean while other material shows break-ins and locked access.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: Items
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

The crowbar is one of the few named tools repeatedly associated with the game's farming-horror identity. It connects exploration, unauthorized entry, secret areas, and potential discovery.

What this changes for players

Its item page can answer the confirmed role and link to break-ins and locked doors, while avoiding fabricated combat damage or upgrade tiers.

What is not confirmed

The acquisition method, durability, inventory slot, upgrade path, eligible doors, detection effect, and combat use are not officially detailed.

Primary source

This update was checked against the official Grave Seasons developer or Steam publication. If a newer first-party announcement changes the fact, this canonical page will be revised rather than duplicated.
