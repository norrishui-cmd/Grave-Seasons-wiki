The Official Item Count Now Exceeds 600

Perfect Garbage reports more than 600 items to discover in Grave Seasons.

What the official update confirms

The total sits alongside farming, foraging, fishing, mining, kitchen and shed crafting, selling, quests, upgrades, and mysterious occult protection. It describes a broad collection system rather than one narrow inventory category.

The wiki can grow into a structured database after names and functions become visible, but each page still needs a specific answer. Count-based placeholder pages remain excluded.
