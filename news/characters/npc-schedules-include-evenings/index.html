Resident Schedules Extend Beyond Work Hours

The full-day system allows residents to appear in social and outdoor locations after dark.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: Characters
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

Official examples include late-night drinking and watching sunset at the docks. This means an absent shop worker may still be found elsewhere rather than disappearing until morning.

What this changes for players

Character guides should eventually show time ranges and alternate locations, while clue walkthroughs should explain how to locate a person when their business is closed.

What is not confirmed

No resident-by-resident timetable is official yet, so exact times and optimal routes are not assigned.

Primary source

This update was checked against the official Grave Seasons developer or Steam publication. If a newer first-party announcement changes the fact, this canonical page will be revised rather than duplicated.
