August 14 Is Now a Historical Grave Seasons Date

The March release-date announcement is superseded by the June Fall 2026 delay.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: Release Date
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

The earlier announcement set August 14, 2026 for PC and consoles and described a planned public Steam demo. The later official update cancelled the demo plan and moved the full game to Fall.

What this changes for players

Historical news pages may retain August 14 to explain the timeline, but canonical release guides should lead with the newer status. This avoids conflicting snippets and inaccurate countdowns.

What is not confirmed

A historical date does not reveal the replacement day. Only a new official announcement can establish the final launch date.

Primary source

This update was checked against the official Grave Seasons developer or Steam publication. If a newer first-party announcement changes the fact, this canonical page will be revised rather than duplicated.
