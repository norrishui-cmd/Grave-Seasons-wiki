Ashenridge NPCs Can Continue Moving at Night

Residents are not universally removed from the map when shops close.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: Gameplay
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

The day-and-night devlog specifically describes NPCs wandering at night, drinking late, and spending time by the docks around sunset. This supports nuanced schedules across a full day.

What this changes for players

Investigation and relationship play may require learning alternative evening locations instead of waiting outside a workplace. It also makes the town feel inhabited beyond business hours.

What is not confirmed

The complete schedule for each named resident, weather effects, seasonal differences, and campaign-specific routine changes remain unknown.

Primary source

This update was checked against the official Grave Seasons developer or Steam publication. If a newer first-party announcement changes the fact, this canonical page will be revised rather than duplicated.
