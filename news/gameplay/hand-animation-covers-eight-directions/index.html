Gameplay News · July 31, 2026

Player Customization Is Hand-Animated Across Eight Directions

Customization assets are built to support the game's multi-directional pixel animation.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: Gameplay
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

The developer says each wearable may require animation in up to eight directions. Individual wearable work can span more than 70 sprite sheets and over 400 triggered animations for actions and reactions.

What this changes for players

This explains why the confirmed launch customization set is finite but highly animated. Hair, clothing, kisses, exhaustion, and murder reactions need consistent directional artwork.

What is not confirmed

The update does not provide an animation viewer, frame counts for every action, mod support, or a promise that all cosmetic combinations behave identically.

Primary source

This update was checked against the official Grave Seasons developer or Steam publication. If a newer first-party announcement changes the fact, this canonical page will be revised rather than duplicated.
