Grave Seasons Has No Automatic Nighttime Hard Stop

The 24-hour design keeps Ashenridge explorable after nightfall instead of forcing sleep at a fixed cutoff.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: Gameplay
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

The official devlog explains that the team did not want night to halt exploration. Players can remain active while NPCs follow evening routines and ominous events unfold in the dark.

What this changes for players

Night becomes a gameplay state for investigation, social schedules, atmosphere, and access planning rather than a decorative filter. Staying out may help locate residents or examine places unavailable during the day.

What is not confirmed

Exact sleep consequences, exhaustion values, stamina recovery, passing-out rules, and clock speed are not published.

Primary source

This update was checked against the official Grave Seasons developer or Steam publication. If a newer first-party announcement changes the fact, this canonical page will be revised rather than duplicated.
