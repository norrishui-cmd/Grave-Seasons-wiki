Guide Planning Must Account for Full-Day NPC Routines

The 24-hour schedule means resident location guides cannot stop at daytime business hours.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: Guide Index
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

The official day-and-night devlog says NPCs can wander at night, drink late, or spend time by the docks at sunset. The town remains explorable after dark rather than forcing the player to sleep at a fixed cutoff.

What this changes for players

Future schedule and clue guides need time-of-day context, alternative resident locations, building access states, and night-specific investigation opportunities. A closed workplace may redirect the player to a resident's evening routine.

What is not confirmed

The developer has not published exact hourly schedules, clock speed, stamina rules, or penalties for staying awake. Those values are not guessed in this guide system.

Primary source

This update was checked against the official Grave Seasons developer or Steam publication. If a newer first-party announcement changes the fact, this canonical page will be revised rather than duplicated.
