Seven Ashenridge Areas Define the Map Guide Framework

The confirmed seven-area hierarchy gives location walkthroughs a stable structure before exact coordinates are available.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: Guide Index
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

The official update names the Farm, Mountainside, Forest, Local District, Commercial District, Coast and Docks, and Mines as major areas. More than 70 interiors sit within the wider town, including unique homes and businesses.

What this changes for players

Walkthroughs can organize routes by major area, then attach residents, interiors, services, resources, clues, and events beneath the correct hub. That creates useful navigation without inventing a complete map.

What is not confirmed

Exact coordinates, travel times, shortcuts, locked entrances, shop hours, and fast-travel rules have not been released. They will be added only after first-party details or repeatable retail testing.

Primary source

This update was checked against the official Grave Seasons developer or Steam publication. If a newer first-party announcement changes the fact, this canonical page will be revised rather than duplicated.
