Guide News · July 31, 2026

Separate Universal Tips From Killer-Specific Routes

The changing killer system requires shared mechanics and campaign spoilers to live on different guide layers.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: Guide Index
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

Official descriptions say the supernatural killer can change between playthroughs, while resident survival and player decisions alter later stories. A clue route that is correct for one campaign may be wrong for another.

What this changes for players

Universal pages should explain farming, time, trust, journal use, crafting, and map navigation. Killer-specific walkthroughs should carry spoiler warnings and their own evidence order, victims, protection steps, and endings.

What is not confirmed

No official complete killer roster or campaign solution set is available. The site does not promote a single resident as the permanent culprit.

Primary source

This update was checked against the official Grave Seasons developer or Steam publication. If a newer first-party announcement changes the fact, this canonical page will be revised rather than duplicated.
