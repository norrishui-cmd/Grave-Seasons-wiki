Map FAQ Adds the Commercial District and Fountain Context

The latest map information identifies the town square, fountain, businesses, and Mayor Fitch's role.

Update snapshot

  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

The Commercial District was the first non-farm area built and the fountain is used for multiple yearly events. Ashenridge also contains seven major areas and more than 70 interiors.

What this changes for players

The FAQ can answer how major regions differ from individual interiors and why the town square matters without pretending to have a final coordinate map.

What is not confirmed

Event names, dates, routes, and the complete business directory remain pending.
