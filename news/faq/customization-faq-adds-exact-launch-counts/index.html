Customization FAQ Adds Exact Launch Counts

The FAQ can now answer the announced counts for wearables, skin tones, hairstyles, hair colors, and clothing colors.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: FAQ
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

Official figures are 35 unique wearables, 25 skin-tone options, 18 hairstyles, 33 ways to color hair, and 14 clothing color options.

What this changes for players

These values turn a vague customization answer into a useful data snapshot while linking to the full customization guide and item database.

What is not confirmed

Names, unlock methods, prices, save presets, and whether options change after launch are not yet published.

Primary source

This update was checked against the official Grave Seasons developer or Steam publication. If a newer first-party announcement changes the fact, this canonical page will be revised rather than duplicated.
