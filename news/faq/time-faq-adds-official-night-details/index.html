Time FAQ Adds Official Night-Exploration Details

The FAQ now reflects that players and NPCs can remain active after dark.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: FAQ
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

The 24-hour devlog confirms no automatic nighttime hard stop and gives examples of late drinking and sunset visits to the docks. Ashenridge is intended to remain explorable after normal business hours.

What this changes for players

Direct answers now distinguish the confirmed full-day concept from unknown clock speed, exhaustion, sleep, and schedule values. This keeps useful night-planning information separate from invented optimization advice.

What is not confirmed

Exact time conversions, resident timetables, stamina costs, passing-out penalties, and sleep recovery values will wait for verified gameplay.

Primary source

This update was checked against the official Grave Seasons developer or Steam publication. If a newer first-party announcement changes the fact, this canonical page will be revised rather than duplicated.
