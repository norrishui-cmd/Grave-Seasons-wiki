Demo FAQ Now Distinguishes Cancelled From Delayed

The planned June 15 public demo was cancelled and has no replacement date.

Update snapshot

  • Topic: FAQ
  • Status: verified official information
  • Reviewed: July 31, 2026
  • Pre-release details may still change

What the official update confirms

The developer said a future demo would depend on studio capacity while the team focuses on launch. PAX or press demonstrations do not equal a public downloadable build.

What this changes for players

The FAQ separates current availability, historical plans, safety guidance, and save-transfer status so players do not chase unofficial downloads.

What is not confirmed

There is no official public demo, new demo date, or carry-over policy.
